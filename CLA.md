# Flowbit Individual Contributor License Agreement

> **Draft.** This agreement has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet and will be
> finalized before Flowbit accepts external contributions. It is modeled on the
> structure of the Apache Software Foundation's Individual CLA.

maintained by the Flowbit maintainers ("we" or "us").

This agreement explains the terms under which you contribute. It protects you
and the Project, and it lets us offer Flowbit under more than one license (see
[D-011](docs/DECISIONS.md#d-011-open-core-licensing-agpl-30--commercial-ee) and
[D-012](docs/DECISIONS.md#d-012-cla-for-external-contributions)). You keep the
copyright in your contributions.

- **"You"** means the individual who signs this agreement.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including modifications or

## 6. How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply on the
pull request with the following comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your signature covers all your future Contributions to the Project.
